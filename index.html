Kid Adventure

Goal: bring the trophy back to the start pedestal. Carry one item at a time, and watch the playfield for your next hint.
Switching heroes reloads their art instantly and respawns you near the start.
Adventure Mode is the steady castle quest. Remix Mode rotates through larger family remix maps.

How to Play

1. Find the sword and bridge near the start routes. 2. Match colored keys to their gates. The white key opens any gate. 3. Watch for the bat. It steals loose items and drops them elsewhere. 4. Defeat dragons with the sword, then bring the trophy home.

Controls

Move: WASD / Arrows
Pick up / Use: E  ·  Drop: Q

Key Bindings

Click a binding button, then press a key. Press Esc to cancel remapping.

Accessibility

Notes

Images are scaled to the entity size after loading from disk. Update the PNGs in each kid folder and refresh to see them in game. Transparent backgrounds look best. Adventure Mode keeps one teachable world so the quest feels consistent. Remix Mode trades that stability for surprise routes and bigger detours.
